Climate Crossroads: Exploratory Graphs

Data: CMIP6 near-surface air temperature (tas), 7 models, 46 IPCC AR6 land regions, 1850 to 2100. All values are warming relative to each model's 1850-1900 average, using the 7-model median (and min/max range) with 10-year smoothing. Back to the main visualization

1. One past, four futures

Average of all 46 land regions (unweighted mean of region medians). Shaded bands show the model range.

2. Which regions warm the most?

Mean warming over 2081-2100 under SSP5-8.5, ranked.

3. Where does the choice of future matter most?

Mean warming over 2081-2100 under low (SSP1-2.6) vs very high (SSP5-8.5) emissions, sorted by the gap between them.

4. When does each region pass +2°C?

First year the smoothed median reaches 2°C of warming, under each scenario. Hover a dot for the exact year.

5. Warming decade by decade

Mean warming per decade for every region. Switch scenarios to compare; the color scale stays fixed so colors are comparable. Hover a cell for the value.

6. Do the models agree?

Each dot is a region. x: median warming over 2081-2100 under SSP5-8.5. y: model spread (max minus min across the 7 models). Hover for the region name.
